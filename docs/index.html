GOOD TO KNOW

Before your run.

What are the controls?

WASD — move, mouse — aim, Q/E/R — abilities, right mouse button — hero ability, F — ultimate, Space — dodge, V — potion, C — interact, Tab — equipment, Esc — pause. Customize keys and auto-aim in Settings.

Is progress saved?

Yes, locally on your device. Export a backup in Settings. Windows and browser editions do not automatically share saves. Changing the website address or clearing browser data can affect access to your profile.

Is this the final release?

This is RC2: a playable release candidate. Systems and automated runs have been tested, but not every combination of heroes, RIFT levels and hardware. Please include your version and reproduction steps when reporting an issue.

Does this website track players?

No analytics or ads have been added. Game history and telemetry stay local and can be disabled. GitHub Pages hosting is subject to GitHub’s privacy policies.
